    Problem Framing and Reframing

    Turn the complaint into a testable problem before anyone starts solving.

    Business
    A sales team says, ‘We need more reps.’ The reframed problem is a handoff delay after demos.
    Coding
    A team says, ‘Rewrite the API.’ The reframed problem is one slow endpoint hurting checkout.
    Start here. When someone says, we need more reps, pause before solving. That sentence already hides a fix. Strip the fix out, name the outcome, and look at the system. Maybe leads are stalling between marketing and sales. The useful question is simple: where does the evidence show the handoff breaking?

    Reading order

    Do not watch them as isolated tips. Use them as a sequence.

    1. I FrameClarify the problem and remove hidden fixes.
    2. II FindObserve the real workflow and locate the right target.
    3. III ProveMap causes, widen options, and test assumptions with evidence.
