# Decision Hygiene and Option Widening

## Use When

- The choice is framed as whether to do one thing or not.
- The team has a favorite solution too early.
- The decision feels emotional or status-driven.
- People are defending their own domain or priority.

## Procedure

1. Name the four decision risks:
   - narrow framing
   - confirmation bias
   - short-term emotion
   - overconfidence
2. Widen options:
   - run the vanishing option test
   - list opportunity costs
   - multitrack two or more solutions
   - find someone who has solved an analogous problem
   - look in other industries or domains
3. Reality-test assumptions.
4. Attain distance:
   - ask what you would advise a peer
   - separate values from immediate emotions
5. Prepare to be wrong:
   - define tripwires
   - define rollback or pivot criteria

## Output

- Option set
- Opportunity costs
- Assumption list
- Chosen option with rationale
- Tripwires and pivot rules

## Watchouts

- Do not vote until options have been widened.
- Do not treat consensus as evidence.
- Do not ignore a weak but recoverable option if it teaches quickly.
